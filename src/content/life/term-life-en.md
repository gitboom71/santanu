# Term Life Insurance

Term life insurance is the simplest and most cost-effective way to secure your family's financial future. Unlike endowment or investment plans, term insurance provides purely financial protection with high sum assured coverage at affordable premiums.

## Key Benefits
* **High Sum Assured:** Substantial coverage at a fraction of the cost of endowment plans.
* **Financial Protection:** Ensures your family can maintain their lifestyle and repay loans if the income earner passes away.
* **Tax Benefits:** Tax savings on premiums paid under Section 80C and tax-free payouts under Section 10(10D).

## How Much Coverage Do You Need?
A general rule of thumb is to choose a sum assured that is **10 to 15 times your annual income**, plus any outstanding debts or major future goals (such as children's higher education).
