# What is Human Life Value (HLV) and Why It Matters

When planning your family's financial security, one of the most important questions is: **How much insurance is enough?**  
The Human Life Value (HLV) method provides a practical way to answer this.

## Why HLV is Important
HLV estimates the economic value of your life to your dependents. It ensures that if something happens to you, your family can maintain their lifestyle, cover liabilities, and meet future goals.

## How HLV is Calculated
The formula is simple:
- **Income Replacement**: 70% of your annual income × remaining working years  
- **Liabilities**: Add outstanding loans or debts  

**HLV = (Income × 70% × Working Years) + Liabilities**

## Example
- Age: 30  
- Retirement Age: 60  
- Annual Income: ₹8,00,000  
- Liabilities: ₹5,00,000  

**HLV = (₹8,00,000 × 70% × 30 years) + ₹5,00,000 = ₹1.25 crore**

This means your family would need approximately ₹1.25 crore of term insurance coverage.
